About Neman Capital

Where real estate, private equity, and innovation converge

Neman Capital is a family office and integrated investment platform operated by the Neman brothers — Brandon, Ryan, Daniel, and Jonathan. Spanning real estate development, private equity, and strategic investments, the platform creates ecosystem value where each division reinforces the others. Real estate developments support portfolio companies, operational expertise informs investment decisions, and strategic positions provide market intelligence across sectors.

Rooted in Los Angeles and guided by a shared belief in community-driven development, the Neman family brings together complementary expertise — from scaling Sweetgreen into a public company, to building CloudKitchens’ enterprise platform, to developing over $1 billion in real estate assets.

The Neman Family

Four brothers, one platform

Brandon Neman
Managing Partner

Brandon leads Neman Capital’s investment strategy, private equity portfolio, and investor relations. He began his career at Sweetgreen, where he saw firsthand how a carefully built brand becomes a cultural movement. He went on to serve as Head of Business Development at CSS and CloudKitchens, where he helped scale their footprint across major U.S. and international markets. B.S. in Business Administration, Georgetown University McDonough School of Business.

Ryan Neman
Co-Founder & Principal, RYDA Ventures

Ryan oversees all acquisitions, finance, and leasing activities for RYDA. With an extensive background in real estate business consulting and restaurant development, he brings a unique talent for sourcing value-add projects and creating strategic partnerships. Throughout his career, Ryan has managed over $1 billion in asset value. B.B.A., Stephen M. Ross School of Business, University of Michigan.

Daniel Neman
Co-Founder & Principal, RYDA Ventures

Daniel oversees development and construction for all RYDA projects. With over a decade of experience, he has spent his career developing and building commercial and mixed-use projects around the U.S., including serving as a Project Manager with Clark Construction. B.B.A., Emory University Goizueta Business School. M.R.E.D., USC Sol Price School of Public Policy.

Jonathan Neman
Co-Founder & CEO, Sweetgreen

Jonathan is Co-Founder and CEO of Sweetgreen (NYSE: SG), the mission-driven restaurant brand serving healthy food at scale with over 200 locations nationwide. He co-founded Sweetgreen in 2007, opening their first location in Georgetown, DC, three months out of college. Named to Forbes “30 Under 30,” Fast Company’s “50 Most Innovative Companies,” and Food & Wine’s “40 Big Food Thinkers 40 and Under.” Born and raised in Los Angeles. B.S., Georgetown University McDonough School of Business.
